Ordinary velcro fights the person using it.

Adaptive clothing swapped zippers and buttons for magnets or velcro to help people with limited dexterity get dressed. Both closures brought their own problems. Magnets can interfere with medical devices. Standard hook-and-loop catches on any fabric it brushes against, wears down with every open-and-close, and often takes real force to pull apart — force that many of the people relying on it don't have to spare.

ADHAL keeps the reliability of hook-and-loop and removes what makes it frustrating: the hooks only grip when they're mechanically told to.

Three positions, one motion.

01

Press

A shear force is applied along the base strip, shifting each hook's root sideways.

02

Stand

The shift pivots the hooks upright, where they catch the loop fibers and hold full grip.

03

Release on command

Shear the other way and the hooks lie flat again. They let go cleanly — no peeling, no snagged threads.

ADHAL isn't a garment — it's a component. We supply adaptive and accommodative fashion brands with a fastener their design teams can drop into existing patterns, so the brand keeps the relationship with its customer and we handle the mechanics.

  • Bulk and licensed supply

    Ordered at production scale, or licensed for a brand's own manufacturing.

  • Fits existing patterns

    Sized and mounted like conventional hook-and-loop tape.

  • A longer usable life

    Because it isn't gripping fabric it shouldn't, the fastener stays effective for more cycles.

  • One less accessibility gap to solve

    A ready answer for brands building toward dignity, comfort, and independence for their customers.
